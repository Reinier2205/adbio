02 / 09
The problem

Medication non-adherence costs lives — and practices.

Patients forget doses. Doctors find out too late. Refill requests come via WhatsApp. There is no clear picture of who is at risk.

📉

No visibility between visits

Doctors have no real-time view of whether patients are taking their medication day to day.

📱

Scattered communication

Refill requests, questions, and follow-ups live across SMS, WhatsApp, and email — untracked and unauditable.

⏳

Reactive, not proactive

Practices only discover adherence issues when a patient deteriorates — not before it happens.

📋

No audit trail

No record of what was communicated, when, or who approved what — a real medico-legal risk.

03 / 09
The solution

One platform. Three portals. Complete care visibility.

DrPat gives every person in the care relationship exactly what they need — without any installation, IT setup, or training.

👨‍⚕️

Doctor portal

Clinical dashboard with live adherence scores, patient risk flags, a refill queue, appointments, and secure messaging.

🧑

Patient portal

Daily medication checklist, 30-day adherence score, refill requests, appointment view, and direct messaging with their doctor.

🛡️

Admin portal

Invite users, assign patients to doctors, manage accounts, and review the full immutable audit log.

Admin portal

Full practice control, built for compliance.

The admin portal gives practice managers complete control over who has access and a permanent record of everything that happens.

  • Invite doctors and patients by email — no self-signup possible
  • Assign patients to specific doctors
  • Activate and deactivate accounts instantly
  • Reset passwords for any user without an email link
  • Resend expired invitation emails in one click
  • Full immutable audit log — clinical, access, and communication events
  • Medication reminder scheduler — generate and send reminders on demand
  • Test account creation for demos and onboarding

Why DrPat

Built for the way practices actually work.

🔒

Invitation-only access

No patient can join without being invited by the practice. The doctor controls exactly who is in the system.

⚡

Nothing to install

Runs in any browser on any device. No app store, no downloads. Patients are up and running in minutes.

📊

Early warning system

Patients below 75% adherence are automatically flagged for review — before they deteriorate or miss appointments.

💬

Secure, on-platform messaging

All care communication stays inside DrPat. No WhatsApp, no personal email. Everything logged and traceable.

📜

Immutable audit trail

Every action — dose recorded, refill approved, message sent — is permanently logged. Medico-legal protection built in.

🌍

Always available

Hosted on Cloudflare's global edge network. No servers to manage, no downtime, no IT overhead for the practice.
