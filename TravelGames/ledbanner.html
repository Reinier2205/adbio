Help & Guide

How it Works

Configure text, choose a font, pick colors, or upload a gallery photo for the background. Tap START LED BANNER to launch full-screen.

Double tap anywhere on the active screen to exit back to setup.

Photo Backgrounds

When you select a photo it uploads automatically to the cloud. Once uploaded the preview updates and sharing works instantly — no compressed thumbnails, full quality.

Sharing Banners

Tap SHARE VIA WHATSAPP to send the full banner setup via a link. The recipient opens it and sees exactly what you configured, including the background photo.

Full-Screen PWA Installation

Android (Chrome):
  • Tap the 3 dots menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen.
iOS (Safari):
  • Tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen.
